## Group class schedule

![(no text)](/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Tula-daily-yoga-class.jpg)

![(no text)](/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/daily-Yoga-Class.jpg)

### Monday – Yin Yoga

Yin Yoga poses apply moderate stress to the connective tissues of the body—the tendons, fasciae, and ligaments—with the aim of increasing circulation in the joints and improving flexibility. A more meditative approach to yoga, its goals are awareness of inner silence, and bringing to light a universal, interconnecting quality.

### Wednesday – Mat Pilates

Pilates teaches balance and control of the body, the flowing water based workout focuses on muscular toning and definition through core stability work with improvements in bone density, joint health, back problems and anxiety.

### Thursday – Hatha Yoga

The entire practice of Hatha yoga is to use the body's own strength to balance the sympathetic / parasympathetic nerves. In this course we will focus on three important steps:breathing, body alignment and meditation, emphasizing the entry into the realm of the Vipassana meditation through breathing and correct stretching.

### Friday – Vinyasa Flow

A dynamic style of yoga that links breath with movement in a continuous, flowing sequence. Each pose transitions smoothly into the next, creating a meditative and physically engaging practice. Emphasizing breath awareness, Vinyasa builds strength, flexibility, and balance while calming the mind.

### Sunday – Water Pilates

Flowing-water based workout focuses on muscular toning and definition through core- stability work.Specific water movements are also supportive for those with back problems, weak joints, and anxiety.
